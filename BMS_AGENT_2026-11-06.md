## Hard safety invariant
- Power stage stays disabled for the entire BMS workstream.
- R3_2_TurnOnLowSides blocked.
- Normal R3_2_SwitchOnPWM blocked.
- TIM1 MOE must be 0 before/after every BMS test.
- No motor commands during BMS work.
- No BMS WRITE commands until explicitly proven necessary and reviewed.
- C/CHRG_MOD untouched unless separately justified.
- Stock 10S battery only for battery-connected BMS tests. No 4S add-on / no 14S while BMS work is open.

## Confirmed hardware facts
- BMS UART is USART3 on ESC: PB10 TX, PB11 RX.
- 115200 8N1 is confirmed on real hardware.
- BMS is silent when only the 4-pin data connector is attached while ESC runs from bench supply.
- BMS responds immediately when stock battery DC+/DC- and data connector are connected normally.
- 76800 produced no responses in the real test.
- Powerlock build stayed MOE=0 throughout the real battery test.

## Confirmed protocol facts
- ESC address 0x20, BMS address 0x22.
- READ command 0x01, READ_RESPONSE 0x04.
- Frame: 5A A5 LEN SRC DST CMD ARG PAYLOAD CKlo CKhi.
- LEN = payload byte count.
- Checksum = ones-complement of sum from LEN through payload.
- Exact Stock DRV126 READ30: 5A A5 01 20 22 01 30 0C 7F FF.
- Stock DRV126 emits READ30; no proven stock activator special-frame is required.

## Confirmed register map
- 0x10 serial, 14 B
- 0x17 firmware version, U16
- 0x18 rated capacity, U16 mAh
- 0x19 actual capacity, U16 mAh
- 0x1B full cycles, U16
- 0x1C charge count, U16
- 0x20 manufacture date, U16
- 0x30 status, U16
- 0x31 remaining capacity, U16 mAh
- 0x32 SoC, U16 %
- 0x33 current, S16 *10 mA (+discharge / -charge)
- 0x34 pack voltage, U16 *10 mV
- 0x35 temperature, U16 encoded
- 0x36 balancing bitmap, U16
- 0x3B SoH, U16 %
- 0x40..0x49 cell 1..10, U16 mV
- 0x51 config straps, U16
- 0x70 activation data, 12 B MCU UID copy

## 19A / 40A question
- Community evidence says missing BMS communication can leave stock battery around ~19 A.
- This is NOT yet proven to require a special write/activation command.
- Exact Stock126 evidence currently shows normal BMS READ polling.
- Hypothesis to prove: normal valid communication itself is sufficient to leave the BMS communication-failsafe current mode.
- Do not add a BMS write solely to chase 40 A without binary evidence.
